About positions and protocol support

Only coordinates actually decoded by the recorder are shown. The current recorder decodes Motorola P25 Phase 1 and L3Harris P25 Phase 1/2 GPS. Other protocols appear automatically when their decoders provide coordinates. An unknown protocol label does not prevent a valid fix from appearing.

Live positions use the recorder’s position log. Their times are approximate; recorded positions use the source transmission time. Trails hold up to 200 observations per radio and 1,000 radios in this tab. Closing or reloading clears this session; the recorder’s recent call history is loaded again on connection.

OpenStreetMap street tiles load automatically with no account or API key. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL. The tile service receives your IP address and the map area you view; radio IDs, names and GPS records are not sent. Choose Offline coordinate grid to stop online tile requests, or Local map tiles to use your own licensed imagery. If online tiles fail, the coordinate grid and radios remain usable.
