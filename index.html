GPS and connection diagnostics

About positions, privacy and protocol support

Only coordinates decoded by your recorder are shown. Motorola/L3Harris P25 GPS and compatible DMR/Hytera GPS builds use the existing position pipeline. This interface does not decode unsupported radio packets or infer why a radio has not sent GPS.

Live times are approximate; recorded coordinates use the source transmission time. Saved history is local to this browser and interface origin. In interface-only mode, closing the page stops collection. With the GPS history plugin, collection continues on the recorder machine while TR Pro is started. Storage may be removed by private browsing, browser cleanup or quota eviction. Export important history.

The browser retains up to 100,000 fixes for your chosen period; the map holds up to 1,000 radios and 2,000 fixes per radio, within a 100,000-fix display limit. Backfill searches up to 50,000 calls at a time. Narrow the time range when a limit is reached. Playback uses actual observations without interpolation; gaps over 5 minutes stay disconnected.

OpenStreetMap street tiles need no account or API key. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL. The service receives your IP address and viewed map area; radio IDs and names are not sent. Choose Offline coordinate grid to stop new online tile requests. Local tiles remain available at tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}.png with appropriate licensing and attribution. Tile usage policy · Report a map issue
